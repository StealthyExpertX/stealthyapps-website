Privacy

FillPro keeps the form data on your device.

Profiles, custom rules, saved fields, and uploads are stored locally in Chrome extension storage. FillPro does not run a cloud profile service and does not collect your form values on a server.

Short version

  • No account is required to use the free extension.
  • Profile data stays in the browser extension on your device.
  • Passwords, payment cards, CVCs, IDs, tax IDs, and one-time codes are skipped by design.
  • Pro payments are handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe.
  • Support emails are separate from local extension data.

Data map

What is stored where.

What FillPro skips

Sensitive fields stay outside the product.

FillPro is not a password manager, payment wallet, tax vault, identity vault, or authentication tool. It tries to avoid password fields, payment card fields, CVCs, bank fields, government IDs, tax IDs, Social Security numbers, and one-time codes.

Website analytics

No ad tracker is required for this site.

The public website is static. It does not need third-party ad tracking to show the product, support pages, privacy page, or contact form.

Rights and requests

How to inspect, delete, or ask about data.

Access

Open FillPro and view your profiles. The local data FillPro uses is visible there because it lives with the extension.

Deletion

Delete profiles inside FillPro, clear extension storage, or uninstall the extension. Local profile data cannot be deleted remotely because it is not stored remotely.

Correction

Edit the saved profile values directly in FillPro. If a field matched incorrectly, send a compatibility report.

Portability

Pro includes export so you can keep a copy of saved FillPro profile data you choose to export.

Sale or sharing

FillPro does not sell personal information and does not share local profile data for advertising.

Contact

Use the privacy topic on the contact page for privacy questions, deletion requests for support emails, or billing-related data questions.

Legal notes

Plain-language policy details.

  • Data controller: Karl, maker of FillPro, United States.
  • Lawful basis for local extension data: the product needs it to perform the autofill function you request.
  • Lawful basis for support messages: your request and any optional details you choose to send.
  • Children: FillPro is not directed at children under 13 in the United States or under 16 where GDPR consent rules apply.
  • International transfers: support email you send may cross United States infrastructure.

Need help?

Ask without sending private form values.

For privacy questions, send a short note with the topic and what you want done. Keep profile values and sensitive documents out of the message.
