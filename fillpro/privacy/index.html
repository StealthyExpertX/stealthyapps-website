Privacy

FillPro saves profiles inside the extension.

The profiles you create are used by the browser extension to fill pages you choose. They are not sent to a FillPro profile server. Exports, support emails, and Pro billing happen only when you start them.

Short version

  • No cloud profile accountCreate profiles and fill pages without making a FillPro account for profile storage.
  • No ad businessSaved profile values are not sold or shared for advertising.
  • You choose what leavesExports, support emails, and Pro billing start only when you choose them.

What FillPro saves

The extension saves what you add.

Profiles

Names, contact details, work links, and custom fields.

FillPro reads these details when you ask it to fill a page.

Rules

Small matching rules for unusual form labels.

Rules help FillPro recognize fields that a site labels in its own way.

Uploads

Files you attach to a profile, like a resume or CSV.

Attach a file only when you want FillPro to match it to an upload field. Remove it anytime.

What can leave the extension

Only a few things leave the extension, and they start with you.

When you export

Export creates a file you control. Treat that file like any other copy of your saved profile.

When you contact support

The contact form sends your message to Stealthy Apps support. A page, field label, and short description are usually enough.

When you buy Pro

ExtensionPay and Stripe handle license and payment details. FillPro does not collect card numbers inside the extension.

Site access

Fill the page you choose.

Fill Page runs after you click it on the page you are viewing. The optional floating button asks for the current site only because it needs to appear there. You can turn it off in FillPro settings.

Sign-ins and payments

Keep sensitive flows in the right place.

Use FillPro for repeated profile fields around a form. Keep passwords, payment cards, CVCs, one-time codes, wallets, and protected identity details with your browser, password manager, wallet, or the site itself.

Your controls

View, edit, export, or delete what you save.

View

Open FillPro to see the profiles, custom fields, rules, and upload matches the extension uses.

Edit

Change saved values inside FillPro whenever a detail, resume, company field, or rule needs an update.

Delete

Delete a profile inside FillPro, clear the extension's storage in your browser, or uninstall FillPro.

Export

Pro includes export for saved profile data you choose to move or back up yourself.

Support records

If you email support, ask for the conversation to be deleted when the issue is finished.

Privacy questions

Use the privacy topic on the contact page for privacy, support-email deletion, or billing questions.

Legal notes

Policy details, without the fog.

  • Data controller: Stealthy Apps, publisher of FillPro, United States.
  • Saved profile details are used to fill the forms you ask FillPro to fill.
  • Support messages are used to answer your question or investigate the report you send.
  • The contact form may send your message through FormSubmit to reach the support inbox.
  • The public website is static and does not need third-party ad tracking to work.
  • Children: FillPro is not directed at children under 13 in the United States or under 16 where GDPR consent rules apply.
  • International transfers: support email you send may cross United States infrastructure.

Need help?

Ask without sending private values.

For privacy questions, send what you need and the email involved. Leave out passwords, payment cards, tax IDs, one-time codes, customer records, and full profile values.
