Privacy

FillPro keeps profile data on your device.

Saved profiles, custom fields, rules, and upload references live in Chrome extension storage. FillPro does not run a cloud profile account or collect form values on a server.

Short version

  • No account is required for the free extension.
  • Profiles stay in the browser extension on your device.
  • Profile values are not sold or shared for advertising.
  • Pro payments are handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe.
  • Support emails are separate from local extension data.

Sensitive fields

Profile filling stays separate from sign-ins and payments.

FillPro is built for repeated profile-style form fields. Password managers, browser autofill, and payment wallets should remain the tools for sign-ins, cards, CVCs, one-time codes, and protected identity details.

Website analytics

No ad tracker is required for this site.

The public website is static. It can show the product page, support page, privacy policy, sitemap, and contact form without third-party ad tracking.

Rights and requests

Inspect, edit, export, or delete your data.

Access

Open FillPro and view your saved profiles. The profile data FillPro uses is visible inside the extension.

Deletion

Delete profiles inside FillPro, clear extension storage, or uninstall the extension. Local profile data is not stored remotely, so deletion happens on the device.

Correction

Edit saved values in FillPro. If a site matched a field incorrectly, send a short site report.

Portability

Pro includes export for saved FillPro profile data you choose to export.

Sale or sharing

FillPro does not sell personal information and does not share local profile data for advertising.

Contact

Use the privacy topic on the contact page for privacy questions, support-email deletion requests, or billing-related data questions.

Legal notes

Plain-language policy details.

  • Data controller: Stealthy Apps, publisher of FillPro, United States.
  • Lawful basis for local extension data: the product needs it to perform the autofill function you request.
  • Lawful basis for support messages: your request and any optional details you choose to send.
  • Children: FillPro is not directed at children under 13 in the United States or under 16 where GDPR consent rules apply.
  • International transfers: support email you send may cross United States infrastructure.

Need help?

Ask without sending private form values.

For privacy questions, send a short note with the topic and what you want done. Keep profile values and sensitive documents out of the message.
