Before installing

What to know before you install.

What does FillPro fill?

Repeated profile fields: names, contact details, company info, work links, long answers, dropdowns, checkboxes, radios, textareas, and upload fields the page exposes to the extension.

Does FillPro send saved profiles to a server?

No. Profiles are saved by the extension in your browser. Exports, support emails, and Pro billing only happen when you choose them.

What site access does it need?

Fill Page runs on the page you choose after you click it. The optional floating button asks for the current site only and can be turned off in FillPro settings.

Does it replace browser autofill or password managers?

No. Keep sign-ins, payment cards, one-time codes, and wallets with tools made for those jobs. FillPro handles the repeated profile fields around them.

How do I report a bad fill?

Send the page, field label, what happened, and what you expected. A short report gives us enough to fix the rule.
