Ask about Pro

Before installing

Straight answers.

What does FillPro fill?

Names, contact details, company fields, work links, long answers, dropdowns, checkboxes, radios, textareas, and uploads like resumes or CSVs when the page exposes a form field FillPro can reach.

Does FillPro send saved profiles to a server?

No. The core workflow uses profiles saved by the extension in your browser. FillPro does not run a cloud profile account for your form values. Exports, support emails, and Pro billing only happen when you choose them.

What site access does it need?

Fill Page runs on the page you are viewing after you click it. The optional floating button is a convenience feature; it asks for access to the current site and can be turned off from FillPro settings.

Does it replace browser autofill or password managers?

No. Keep sign-ins, payment cards, one-time codes, and wallets with tools made for those jobs. FillPro handles the repeated profile fields around them.

How do I report a bad fill?

Send the URL, field label, what FillPro did, and what should have happened. Leave out private values. Good reports become regression tests and fixes.
