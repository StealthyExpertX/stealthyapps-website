Questions people ask before installing

Plain answers.

What does FillPro fill?

Profile details, custom fields, dropdowns, checkboxes, radios, textareas, contact fields, job-application fields, support fields, and local upload fields when the page exposes form controls FillPro can reach.

Does FillPro send my profile to a server?

No cloud profile account is needed for the core workflow. Saved profiles, smart rules, custom fields, and upload references stay in browser extension storage unless you export them.

Does it replace browser autofill or a password manager?

No. Use FillPro for repeated profile-style fields. Keep sign-ins, payment cards, CVCs, and one-time codes with browser autofill, your password manager, your payment wallet, or the site controls built for those flows.

How do I report a bad fill?

Send the page, the field label, what FillPro did, and what should have happened. That is enough to turn a bad match into a better rule or engine fix.
