Questions

What people ask before installing.

Can I use Skip Retyping for free?

Yes. Free includes up to 3 saved profiles, Fill Page, Smart Rules, upload matching, and Undo. No account or card is needed to start.

What does Skip Retyping fill?

Repeated profile fields: names, contact details, company info, work links, long answers, dropdowns, checkboxes, radios, textareas, and upload fields the page exposes to the extension.

Does Skip Retyping send saved profiles to a server?

No cloud profile account is needed. Profiles are saved in your browser. Filling shares entered values and files with the destination website, which may autosave before submission. Exports and support share what you choose. Pro checkout sends license and payment details, not saved profile values.

What site access does it need?

Fill Page runs on the page you choose after you click it. The optional in-page button and auto-fill request access only for sites you enable, and both can be turned off in Settings.

Does it replace browser autofill or password managers?

No. Keep sign-ins, payment cards, one-time codes, and wallets with tools made for those jobs. Skip Retyping handles the repeated profile fields around them.

What happens when a field is not a clear match?

The field may stay blank instead of receiving a guess. After each fill, review what changed and use Undo if the page needs another pass.
