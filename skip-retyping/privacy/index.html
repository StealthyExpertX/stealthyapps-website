Privacy

Your saved profiles stay in your browser.

By default, Skip Retyping fills only when you run Fill Page. If you enable auto-fill for a site, it can fill detected forms there with your default profile. Saved profile values are not sent to a Skip Retyping server.

Short version

  • No profile accountCreate profiles and fill pages without making an account for profile storage.
  • No ad businessSaved profile values are not sold or shared for advertising.
  • You choose what leavesExports and support messages include only what you choose. Checkout sends license and payment details, not profile values.

What Skip Retyping saves

Skip Retyping saves only what you add.

Profiles

Names, contact details, work links, and custom fields.

Skip Retyping uses these details inside the extension and adds them to a page only when you choose Fill Page.

Rules

Small matching rules for unusual form labels.

Rules help Skip Retyping recognize fields that a site labels in its own way.

Uploads

Files you attach to a profile, like a resume or CSV.

Attach a file only when you want Skip Retyping to match it to an upload field. Remove it anytime.

Exports, support, and billing

Exports, support, and Pro checkout.

When you export

Export creates a file you control. Treat that file like any other copy of your saved profile.

When you contact support

The contact form sends only the message you submit. If you uninstall Skip Retyping, the same page may open for an optional anonymous note.

When you buy Pro

ExtensionPay checks the Pro license, and Stripe handles payment. Skip Retyping does not collect card numbers or send saved profile values during checkout. On Firefox, Skip Retyping asks before connecting your billing email, sign-in, and payment-status data.

This website can ask whether Skip Retyping is installed and read its version so an install button can become Open Skip Retyping. The check cannot read profiles, rules, uploads, or form values.

Site access

Fill the page you choose.

The optional in-page button and auto-fill use access only on sites you grant. You can disable either feature in Settings.

Sign-ins and payments

Use your usual sign-in and payment tools.

Use Skip Retyping for repeated profile fields around a form. Keep passwords, payment cards, CVCs, one-time codes, wallets, and protected identity details with your browser, password manager, wallet, or the site itself.

Salary, work-authorization, demographic, and health answers stay blank during Fill Page. Fill one only when you choose that field.

Your controls

View, edit, export, or delete what you save.

View

Open Skip Retyping to see the profiles, custom fields, rules, and upload matches the extension uses.

Edit

Change saved values inside Skip Retyping whenever a detail, resume, company field, or rule needs an update.

Delete

Delete a profile inside Skip Retyping, clear the extension's storage in your browser, or uninstall Skip Retyping.

Export

Export is available on every plan; Pro adds backup import.

Support records

If you email support, ask for the conversation to be deleted when the issue is finished.

Legal notes

Who handles what.

  • Controller: Stealthy Apps, an independent software publisher based in Maine, United States. Use the privacy contact form to reach the controller.
  • The use of information received from Google APIs by Skip Retyping adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Profile values are used in the extension to edit profiles, create an export you request, and fill a page you choose. Stealthy Apps does not receive those values during normal filling.
  • ExtensionPay receives billing-account and license-status details when you use Pro billing. Stripe receives payment details. FormSubmit and email providers carry a support message only when you send one.
  • Website hosting and security systems may process ordinary request details such as IP address, browser information, requested URL, and time to deliver and protect the site. Skip Retyping does not add advertising or behavioral-analytics trackers.
  • Local extension data stays until you delete it, clear extension storage, or uninstall. Support records stay only as long as needed to answer the request, prevent abuse, or meet legal duties. Billing providers keep transaction records under their policies and applicable tax and accounting rules.
  • Where privacy law applies, you may ask to access, correct, delete, restrict, or receive the personal information Stealthy Apps controls, and you may object to or withdraw consent-based processing. UK users may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
  • Support and billing information may be processed in the United States or other countries used by the named providers. Skip Retyping is not directed at children under 13 in the United States or under 16 where GDPR consent rules apply.
