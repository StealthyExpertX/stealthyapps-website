Before install

Short answers.

What does FillPro fill?

Profile details, custom fields, dropdowns, checkboxes, radios, textareas, contact fields, job-application fields, support fields, and local upload fields when the page exposes accessible form controls.

Why not auto-submit?

Review is part of the product. A form should not leave the browser until the person filling it has seen what changed.

Does it need broad site access?

The core fill flow uses active-tab access. Optional all-sites access is only for optional floating-button and auto-fill behavior.

How do I report a site?

Send the page, field label, what happened, and what you expected. That is enough to turn a bad fill into a better rule or engine fix.
