# FillPro

Private Chrome form autofill for job applications, signup forms, onboarding pages, intake forms, demo requests, vendor forms, and repeated business forms.

- Product: FillPro
- Brand: Stealthy Apps
- Website: https://stealthyapps.com/
- Canonical product page: https://stealthyapps.com/apps/fillpro/
- Current release: 1.0.0
- Last updated: 2026-06-12

FillPro fills the web forms your browser still makes you type by hand. It stores profiles locally in Chrome extension storage, lets you choose the profile before filling, and intentionally skips passwords, payment cards, CVCs, government IDs, tax IDs, and one-time codes.

## Best fit

- Job applications and applicant profiles.
- Customer intake and onboarding forms.
- Demo requests and vendor signups.
- Internal admin forms and profile pages.
- Repeated business forms that ask for the same details again and again.

## Privacy boundary

FillPro does not run a cloud profile service. Saved profiles, rules, custom fields, and upload references stay on the user's device. Billing for Pro is handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe.

## Pricing

- Free: 3 saved profiles, local filling, on-page picker, smart rules, custom fields, and local upload matching.
- Pro monthly: $3.99/month.
- Pro yearly: $29.99/year.
- Pro lifetime: $39.99 one time.
- Trial: 3 days, no card required.
