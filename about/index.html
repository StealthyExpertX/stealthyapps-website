About

Stealthy Apps makes focused browser tools.

FillPro is the first public product: a Chrome form filler for job applications, onboarding pages, demo requests, vendor forms, and the repeat typing that shows up in real work.

The promise is small on purpose.

FillPro should save time without trying to become your password manager, payment wallet, identity vault, or cloud CRM. Profiles live in your browser. Sensitive fields stay out of scope. The tool stays understandable.

How it is built

Useful first, polished second, honest always.

Local by default

Saved profiles, rules, and uploaded files stay in extension storage on your device. Stealthy Apps cannot read them from a server because they are not synced through one.

Built for messy forms

Modern sites use React, Vue, Angular, Shadow DOM, custom dropdowns, segmented fields, and odd labels. FillPro is tuned around those rough edges.

Support that feeds the product

If a site fills badly, a clear report can become a better rule, safer field detection, or a sharper product boundary.

Today

One public product: FillPro.

The site is intentionally focused on the extension that is ready now. No fake product wall, no pretend suite, no vague roadmap dressed up as a launch.

Later

Room for more tools.

The site uses shared page patterns, clean metadata, and a simple sitemap so future tools can get their own pages without rebuilding the whole thing. FillPro stays the main story until another product earns its spot.
