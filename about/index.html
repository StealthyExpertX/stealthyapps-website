About

FillPro is the form filler I wanted when browser autofill ran out of road.

I am Karl. I build FillPro for the forms that ask for more than name, email, and address: job applications, onboarding pages, demos, vendor forms, and all the repeat typing that shows up in real work.

The promise is small on purpose.

FillPro should save time without trying to become your password manager, payment wallet, identity vault, or cloud CRM. Profiles live in your browser. Sensitive fields stay out of scope. The tool stays understandable.

How it is built

Useful first, polished second, honest always.

Local by default

Saved profiles, rules, and uploaded files stay in the extension storage on your device. I cannot read them from a server because they are not synced through one.

Built for messy forms

Modern sites use React, Vue, Angular, Shadow DOM, custom dropdowns, segmented fields, and odd labels. FillPro is tuned around those rough edges.

Support from the person building it

If a site fills badly, the fix goes through me. A clear report can become a better rule, safer field detection, or a sharper product boundary.

Today

The site is intentionally focused on the extension that is ready now. No fake product wall, no pretend suite, no vague roadmap dressed up as a launch.
