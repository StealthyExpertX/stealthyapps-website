# About FillPro

FillPro is built by Karl as a focused Chrome form filler for repeated web forms.

The product exists because normal browser autofill is good at basic contact details but often falls short on job applications, onboarding pages, intake forms, vendor signups, demo requests, and internal business forms.

## Product principles

- Keep profile data local.
- Fill useful non-sensitive fields quickly.
- Skip passwords, payment cards, IDs, tax IDs, and one-time codes.
- Make pricing and privacy boundaries clear.
- Support messy real-world sites instead of only perfect demo forms.

## Current product

FillPro is the only public product on the site right now. The site structure is ready for future tools later, but the home page and navigation stay focused on FillPro until another product is ready.
