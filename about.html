About

Hello, I'm Karl.

I'm Karl. I've spent the last decade building tools, automation, and product UI for real work. Stealthy Apps is where I ship the privacy-first versions I want to use myself.

Ground rules

One builder. Clear standards.

  • Built one project at a time.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or forced sign-up just to try something.
  • Keep data on your device whenever the product can work that way.

Why I'm doing this

Too many useful tools come with too much junk.

I got tired of software that wrapped simple jobs in tracking, bloated permissions, upsells, and friction. I would rather build tools that feel clear, useful, and respectful the first time you open them.

Over the years I have worked on automation, data-heavy tooling, internal workflows, and studio-facing software. That background is why I care so much about edge cases, clean UX, and shipping things I can actually support.

How I work

What matters here.

I build, test, support, and price this work like something I expect people to keep using.

Ten years in tools

I have spent about a decade building scripts, internal tools, extensions, automation, and interface-heavy software for real teams and real workflows.

Tested before release

I do not publish something because the page looks ready. It gets manual testing, ugly edge-case checks, and the kind of real use that finds annoying failures early.

Straight pricing

If a product has a paid tier, it should be easy to understand, easy to cancel, and tied to clearly useful extras. No pricing games and no gutted free version.

Direct support

If you send a message, you are talking to me. I read the feedback myself, answer it myself, and use it to decide what deserves to be fixed or built next.
