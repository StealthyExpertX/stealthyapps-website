Fix the common stuff fast. Send the weird forms to Stealthy Apps.

FillPro is built for the busy middle of the web: job applications, signup forms, intake pages, vendor forms, and repeat business forms. Some sites still need a sharper rule, and those reports are useful.

Report a bad fill Check privacy

FillPro will not fill everything.

It intentionally avoids passwords, payment fields, CVCs, government IDs, tax IDs, and one-time codes. It also cannot reach fields inside cross-origin iframes because the browser blocks that for good security reasons.

Quick checks

Try these before filing a report.

  1. Open the page you want to fill, then open FillPro from the extension icon or side panel.
  2. Pick the correct profile before filling, especially if you use work, personal, and applicant profiles.
  3. If the site has tabs, accordions, or a multi-step form, open the section before filling it.
  4. If a dropdown is searchable, click into it once and try FillPro again.
  5. If the page changes after login or after a first step, refresh the page and run FillPro again.

Compatibility

What FillPro is designed to handle.

Modern web apps

React, Vue, Angular, custom selects, comboboxes, masked inputs, date fields, and forms that update while you type.

Repeated work forms

Job applications, intake forms, demo requests, vendor signups, customer onboarding, profile pages, and internal admin forms.

Files and profiles

Resume-style uploads, saved local profiles, custom fields, field-learning suggestions, import, export, and undo for the last fill.

Billing

Pro access, restore, cancel, refunds.

Billing runs through ExtensionPay and Stripe. Do not send card details. For access or refund questions, send the purchase email, the plan, and what you expected to happen.

Privacy

Local data stays local.

Your profiles live in Chrome extension storage on your device. To clear them, remove them inside FillPro or uninstall the extension. Support emails are separate and should stay light on private detail.
