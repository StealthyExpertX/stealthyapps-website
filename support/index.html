Quick checks

Try these five quick checks.

  1. Open the page you want to fill, then open FillAhead from the extension icon or side panel.
  2. Pick the correct profile before filling, especially if you use work, personal, and applicant profiles.
  3. If the site has tabs, accordions, or a multi-step form, open the section before filling it.
  4. If a dropdown is searchable, open it once and try FillAhead again.
  5. If the page changes after login or after a first step, refresh the page and run FillAhead again.

Common questions

Short answers before you contact support.

Why did FillAhead miss a field?

The field may be hidden, loaded after the page opens, or labeled in an unusual way. Open the form section, try again, then add a narrow Smart Rule if the label still needs help.

Where do I manage or restore Pro?

Open FillAhead and find the plan card below your profiles. Use Manage billing for an active subscription, Update billing after a failed renewal, or Try again if the billing check was interrupted. To recover Pro on another browser or profile, open the Pro window and choose Restore purchase.
