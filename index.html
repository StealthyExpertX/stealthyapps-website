Why it exists

Browser autofill still leaves too much work behind.

Default autofill is fine for a shipping address. FillPro picks up the rest: job history, company details, repeated messages, local files, odd labels, and the same custom fields showing up again next week.

FillPro is for the rest of the form.

  • Named profiles for different roles, clients, or test data.
  • Smart rules for awkward field labels.
  • Local upload matching for resumes, cover letters, and PDFs.
  • Undo when a form needs a second look.

Made for boring forms that waste real time.

Job applications

Reuse contact details, profile links, resume uploads, and the fields you keep seeing on Workday-style forms.

Signup and onboarding

Fill normal account, team, vendor, and intake forms from reusable local profiles.

Business forms

Privacy boundary

Local profiles. Clear control.

FillPro keeps saved profiles, smart rules, and uploads in extension-managed local browser storage. It is built for repeated profile details, not broad account or payment automation.

  • Designed for names, contact details, company details, messages, dropdowns, and uploads.
  • Review the page before any form leaves your browser.
  • Optional site access is off by default and only used for optional floating-button or auto-fill behavior.
