Why it exists

Browser autofill still leaves too much work behind.

Default autofill is fine for a shipping address. It is not great for the rest of the form: job history, company details, repeated messages, local files, odd labels, and the same custom fields showing up again next week.

FillPro is for the rest of the form.

  • Named profiles for different roles, clients, or test data.
  • Smart rules for awkward field labels.
  • Local upload matching for resumes, cover letters, and PDFs.
  • Undo when a form needs a second look.

Made for boring forms that waste real time.

Business forms

Keep a company profile for demo requests, partner intake, procurement forms, and support pages.

Privacy boundary

Private by default, plain about limits.

FillPro keeps saved profiles, smart rules, and uploads in extension-managed local browser storage. It does not collect form values in the background and it does not try to handle sensitive fields.

  • Skips passwords, payment fields, CVCs, SSNs, tax IDs, government IDs, and one-time codes.
  • Does not bypass CAPTCHA, anti-bot checks, login walls, payment iframes, or browser-native PDF viewers.
  • Optional site access is off by default and only used for optional floating-button or auto-fill behavior.
