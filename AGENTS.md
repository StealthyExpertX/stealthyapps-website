## Product Positioning

FillPro is a private Chrome form autofill extension for job applications, signup forms, onboarding pages, intake forms, demo requests, vendor forms, and repeated business forms.

Profiles, rules, custom fields, and upload references stay local in Chrome extension storage. FillPro intentionally skips passwords, payment cards, CVCs, government IDs, tax IDs, Social Security numbers, and one-time codes.
